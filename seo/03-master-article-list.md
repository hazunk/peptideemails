## Positioning (locked for all articles) — updated 2026-08-07

**Preferred vendors, in order:**
1. **FluentCRM (self-hosted)** — our primary recommendation. Own the data, the sending, and the deliverability end-to-end; nothing to ban.
2. **Sendlane** — preferred hosted platform for peptide / high-risk senders.
3. **Omnisend** — used only occasionally (adjacent supplement / GLP-1 DTC that wants ecommerce-native + bundled SMS). **We rarely use it — do not position it as a default.**

- **Klaviyo, Mailchimp, Mailgun = the weaker/riskier options** we compare against and win the argument over.
- In comparison articles, the recommended path is almost always **FluentCRM or Sendlane**; mention Omnisend as a situational option, not the hero.
- ⚠️ **Site copy conflict to fix:** the live homepage says Omnisend "is not built for peptides." That's consistent with rarely using it — but make sure the blog and site don't contradict on whether Omnisend is ever acceptable.
- Every article stays **compliant** (research-only framing, no disease/dosage claims) — we model what we sell.
