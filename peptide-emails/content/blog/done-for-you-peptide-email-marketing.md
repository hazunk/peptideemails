| Flow | What It Does |

Each flow is written specifically for [email marketing for peptide brands](/blog/email-marketing-for-peptide-brands) — not adapted from a supplement template with the word "peptide" swapped in. That distinction is the whole business.

## The Infrastructure We Run

Platform choice isn't a preference for us — it's a compliance decision. We build on self-hosted FluentCRM, which means you own your data and your sending infrastructure outright. Nobody can freeze a list you host yourself. For brands that want a hosted option with strong deliverability, we use Sendlane. Occasionally, for adjacent supplement DTC brands, we'll bring in Omnisend. What we don't do is put a peptide brand on Klaviyo or Mailchimp. Both platforms have suspended peptide and GLP-1 accounts with no warning and no export window — the list you spent two years building, gone in an afternoon. We covered the full comparison in [the platforms we recommend](/blog/best-email-platforms-peptide-supplement-brands), and it's not a close call.

Every account we run gets an isolated sending subdomain, fully authenticated with SPF, DKIM, and DMARC. That's not optional polish — it's what keeps your primary domain's reputation insulated from your email sending, and it's what keeps inbox placement high when your list scales into six figures of sends per month.

## How We Keep You Compliant

Every single email — flow, campaign, one-off — gets reviewed against 21 CFR 201.128 before it ever reaches a send queue. That's disease claims stripped out, dosage guidance removed, consumption testimonials rewritten into research-use language. We keep an audit-ready archive of every email we've ever sent for you, timestamped, so if a question ever comes up you have the paper trail immediately.

And you approve every email before it sends. Not after. Not "you can review it if you want." Every send goes through you first. We're the compliance layer, not a replacement for your judgment on your own brand.

This matters more than it sounds like it does. A single disease claim in a single email is what turns into an FDA warning letter. We've built our entire review process around never letting that happen on our watch.
